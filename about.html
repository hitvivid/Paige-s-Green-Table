Nice to meet you

Hi, I'm Paige.

I'm not a vegetarian. My husband is.

When we started sharing meals, I quickly realized that cooking for someone you love means more than following a recipe. It means learning what makes them feel cared for. For him, that meant learning to make vegetarian food that is truly satisfying, not just a plate of sides.

At first it wasn't easy. Busy weeks left little time to plan, shop, and cook. Some dishes fell flat. Specialty ingredients were hard to find. But I kept testing, adjusting, and tasting until I had a collection of recipes that we both love, and a rhythm that makes cooking feel manageable instead of overwhelming.

Along the way I learned where to shop around New Jersey and New York, how to prep ahead so weeknights feel calm, and which restaurants are worth booking for a birthday, an anniversary, or any moment worth celebrating.

Why this site exists

If you're a vegetarian in the NJ/NY area, or you love one, I want to make your life a little easier and your table a little more joyful. Every recipe here was made with love and tested in a real, busy kitchen.
