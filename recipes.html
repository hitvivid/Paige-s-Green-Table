Main Dish

Vegetarian Mapo Tofu

Silky tofu simmered in a savory, spicy Sichuan-style sauce with mushrooms and aromatic seasonings.

Quick Metadata
Prep Time: 15 mins
Cook Time:20 mins
Total Time:35 mins
Yields:4 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1
    Prepare the Tofu

    Drain the tofu, cut it into bite-size cubes, and gently simmer it in hot water for 2 minutes. Drain and set aside.

  2. 2
    Build the Sauce

    Warm oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and cook until softened, then stir in the garlic and doubanjiang for 1 minute.

  3. 3
    Simmer the Tofu

    Add the vegetable broth and tofu. Simmer gently for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring carefully so the tofu keeps its shape.

  4. 4
    Thicken and Serve

    Stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook until the sauce thickens. Serve over rice and garnish with sliced scallions and Sichuan pepper.

Chef's Note

Add the cornstarch slurry gradually and stir gently. For a milder dish, reduce the doubanjiang and finish with sesame oil instead of extra chili oil.

Sauce

Creamy Fennel Sauce

A smooth, savory sauce made with roasted fennel, garlic, and cream, perfect for pasta, vegetables, or grains.

Quick Metadata
Prep Time:10 mins
Cook Time:25 mins
Total Time:35 mins
Yields:2 cups

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1
    Soften the Fennel

    Heat the olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the sliced fennel and cook until softened and lightly golden, about 8 minutes.

  2. 2
    Add the Aromatics

    Stir in the garlic and cook for 1 minute, until fragrant.

  3. 3
    Simmer and Blend

    Add the vegetable broth and simmer for 10 minutes. Blend until smooth using an immersion blender.

  4. 4
    Finish the Sauce

    Stir in the cream, salt, and pepper. Warm gently without boiling, then serve over pasta, vegetables, or grains.

Chef's Note

For a dairy-free version, replace the heavy cream with unsweetened oat cream or cashew cream.

Quick Metadata

Bake Time:45 mins
Yields:1 loaf

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1
    Mix the Dough

    Combine the starter and water, then add the flour and salt. Mix until no dry flour remains.

  2. 2
    Bulk Ferment

    Cover and let the dough rise at room temperature for 4 to 6 hours, folding it gently every 30 minutes during the first 2 hours.

  3. 3
    Shape and Proof

    Shape the dough into a round loaf and place it in a floured proofing basket. Refrigerate overnight.

  4. 4
    Bake the Loaf

Noodle

Handmade Fresh Noodles

Springy homemade noodles made with a simple flour-and-water dough, ready for soups, stir-fries, or sesame sauce.

Quick Metadata
Prep Time:30 mins
Rest Time:30 mins
Cook Time:5 mins
Yields:4 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1
    Make the Dough

    Mix the flour and salt. Add the water gradually and knead until a firm, smooth dough forms.

  2. 2
    Rest the Dough

    Cover the dough and let it rest for 30 minutes. Knead it briefly again before rolling.

  3. 3
    Roll and Cut

    Roll the dough thin, dust it with cornstarch, fold it loosely, and cut it into even noodles.

  4. 4
    Boil and Serve

    Boil the noodles in salted water for 3 to 5 minutes. Drain and serve immediately with your favorite sauce or broth.

Chef's Note

Keep the cut noodles well dusted with cornstarch so they do not stick together while you prepare the cooking water.
