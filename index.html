A non-vegetarian's love letter to vegetarian cooking

Vegetarian cooking, simplified

Delicious vegetarian food, made simple

Busy week? Cooking for someone you love? I'll help you eat well, shop smart, and prep with ease, right here in New Jersey and New York.

Eating vegetarian should feel joyful, not exhausting. I know how hard it is to cook something truly delicious when your days are packed. So I tested, tweaked, and kept only the recipes that taste amazing. Then I worked out where to buy the ingredients locally and how to prep efficiently.

This site is my way of sharing that love with you, whether you're cooking for yourself or for a vegetarian family member or friend.
