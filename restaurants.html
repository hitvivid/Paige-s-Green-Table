Our Favorite Restaurants

Some moments deserve a table set by someone else. These are my favorite places in the NJ/NY area for celebrations, chosen for flavor, atmosphere, and how well they treat vegetarian guests.

Note: every restaurant listed has been tried by us and found to be excellent for vegetarian options.

$$$

Dirty Candy, NY

Best for birthday/anniversary; also great for casual dining.

Changing seasonal menu. Great pairing for wine.

Avant Garden, NY

A unique dining experience with a focus on sustainable and locally-sourced ingredients.

$$

Pure Thai Cookhouse, NY

Authentic Thai cuisine with a modern twist.

Must try dish: Green Curry.

Azuma Soba, NJ

Traditional handmade fresh soba noondles. Same price for noodle 300g vs 100g.

$

Cafe Buhamara, NJ

A hidden gem with European style for great turkish coffee and light bites.

Great for brunch and family gatherings.

Pho Hanoi Corner, NJ

Authentic Vietnamese pho which is a local favorite.

Limited seating.Accept researvation for vegetarian options.
